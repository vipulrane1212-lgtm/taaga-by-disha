WebP Frame Extractor

Convert your 6-second UGC video directly in the browser into an optimized, numbered sequence of 180 WebP frames (frame_0001.webp to frame_0180.webp) ready for Shopify asset upload.

Drag and drop your 6s UGC MP4/MOV video here, or click to browse
Shopify Deployment Steps:
  1. Extract the downloaded .zip archive. You will have frame_0001.webp to frame_0180.webp.
  2. Go to your Shopify Admin > Content > Files (or Theme > Edit Code > Assets).
  3. Drag all 180 WebP files into the Shopify Files dashboard.
  4. In your Theme Customizer > Hero Video Scrub, paste the CDN URL pattern or leave as default.
